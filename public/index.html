Every crash bet loses 1% of its stake on average, whatever the target, so the expected result is −1% of what a player wagered. The score is how many standard deviations of pure luck a player's profit sits above that, given their bet sizes and targets. Odds is how rare that is for one player. Lucky by chance is how many of the qualifying players you'd expect to reach that score by luck alone: well below 1 means the result is hard to put down to luck.
